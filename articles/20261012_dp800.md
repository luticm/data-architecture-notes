# DP-800: SQL AI Developer Associate

I passed **DP-800: SQL AI Developer Associate** on Oct 09th 2026. <br/>
This follows **DP-300: Azure Database Administrator Associate**, which I completed back on May 30th.

## Thoughts on DP-800

For someone with a decent SQL Server background and previous database certifications, DP-300 felt like a natural way to validate existing knowledge, identify gaps, and keep my skills current.

**DP-800 was a different ball game**. It pushes into areas beyond traditional database administration and development, touching on integrations with Azure services, GitHub, MCP, CI/CD, Data API Builder (DAB), and, of course, many AI scenarios around SQL Server.<br/>
That is one of the reasons I still see a lot of value in certifications, in special this one: they force you to step outside the areas you may work with every day.


## How I prepare for exams

1. Schedule the exam. Nothing like a little pressure to keep things moving.
2. Start with self-learning to guide me through the exam topics.
3. <u>Take quick notes</u> on syntax, important details, and small things that you may not have time to test or get too comfortable with before the exam.
   1. Copy small snippets from docs/learn references and **add small comments yourself**. If AI just creates a summary on your behalf, I doubt it will be that efficient when you review it.
4. Build quick demos for specific features, such as JSON, fuzzy matching, and regex. I don't like to memorize syntax, especially with all the help AI gives us these days, but during the exam you're on your own.
   1. Here AI is your friend, straightforward to have rudimentary examples that you can play with quickly. Don't let AI create large .sql scripts, it defeats the purpose.
   2. Control your inner geek, for instance, if you find yourself digging into execution plans for vector search, add that to your internals/mastering TO DO list, go back to learning path and focus on the outcome (get certified).
5. Go deeper on topics that are central to the exam and where experience is lacking
   1. For DP-800, for example, even having experience, I put extra work into vector search, embeddings, hybrid search, and RAG.
   2. Also, don't ignore DAB (Data API Builder).
6. On exam day review those notes and examples. Wake up early, grab a coffee, spend a couple of hours doing a quick review and go get certified.
